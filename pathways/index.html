About

Before a medicine or vaccine can be sold in a country, that country's national regulator has to authorise it. Many regulators in lower- and middle-income countries have limited staff and resources, which can slow approvals and delay patients' access to new medicines.

Reliance procedures offer a faster route. Rather than repeating the whole assessment, a national regulator can build on work already done by a trusted reference authority.
